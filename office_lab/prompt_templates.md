# Microsoft 365 Copilot — prompt templates by role

Copy, fill the brackets, paste. Each template follows Task + Context + Constraints + Output Format.

## All staff — Outlook
> Draft a professional email to [team / supplier / customer] about [topic]. Context: [two sentences on what happened]. Tone: [formal / collaborative / concise]. Keep it under 150 words. End with one specific ask and a date.

> Summarize this email thread. List: decisions made, open questions, action items with owners. Group by priority.

## Analysts — Excel
> Analyze the selected table. Identify the top 3 [metric] by [dimension], flag anomalies, and suggest the single most useful chart for an executive audience.

> Create a formula in column [X] that [describes the calculation], handling blank cells and returning "n/a" when the denominator is zero.

> Find duplicate rows based on [columns] and rows where [column] is blank. Do not delete anything; add a "flag" column.

## Managers / PMs — PowerPoint and Word
> Create a [N]-slide deck for [audience] on [topic]. Slides: status, risks, decisions needed, timeline, resource asks, next steps. One idea per slide; add speaker notes.

> Rewrite this section so a non-technical VP can understand it. Keep every number. Under 120 words.

> Turn these bullet notes into a one-page project brief with sections: goal, scope, timeline, risks, owner.

## Meeting attendees — Teams
> Summarize this meeting. List: key decisions, open questions, action items with owners and dates. Then tell me anything that was assigned to me.

> What did the group disagree about, and was it resolved?
